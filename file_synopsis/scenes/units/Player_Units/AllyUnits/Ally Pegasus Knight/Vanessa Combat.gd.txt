FILE: scenes/units/Player_Units/AllyUnits/Ally Pegasus Knight/Vanessa Combat.gd

1. CLASS
No class_name. `extends "res://scenes/units/Unit_Scripts/Combat Unit.gd"` (Combat_Unit, Node2D).
Combat-animation controller for the pegasus knight's battle scene.

2. WHAT IT DOES
Overrides only the miss-SFX/dodge hook and the death-complete signal for the pegasus knight's battle
animation. Called by the combat scene's AnimationPlayer method tracks.

3. FUNCTIONS
- signal play_enemy_dodge_anim: opponent dodge cue on miss.
- play_miss_sound(): random "Attack Miss 1..2" from BattlefieldInfo.battle_sounds; emits
  play_enemy_dodge_anim.
- death_anim_signal(): emits base death_anim_done so the Combat_Screen proceeds after the death anim.

4. POSSIBLE IMPROVEMENT
- Both methods duplicate the base Combat_Unit verbatim; the subclass is redundant — attach Combat
  Unit.gd directly.
- Same randf_range->int off-by-one; use randi_range.

5. RELATION TO DEATH OR TAXATION
SCRAP. No per-unit combat-animation scenes or miss mechanic in DoT. Keep only the general idea that
the view reacts to sim signals (unit_destroyed) with a cue, implemented once and data-driven.
