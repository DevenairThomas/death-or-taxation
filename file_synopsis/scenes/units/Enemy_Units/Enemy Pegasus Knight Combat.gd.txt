FILE: scenes/units/Enemy_Units/Enemy Pegasus Knight Combat.gd

1. CLASS
No class_name. extends "res://scenes/units/Unit_Scripts/Combat Unit.gd" (Combat_Unit subclass). Enemy-side combat-cutscene actor for the Enemy Pegasus Knight (its Enemy Pegasus Knight Combat.tscn). Byte-for-byte equivalent to the other enemy *Combat scripts.

2. WHAT IT DOES
AI-side audiovisual glue for the flyer's duel: attack/hit/draw/put-away/miss/crit/death sounds and death-anim signal. Reads BattlefieldInfo.combat_ai_unit for weapon SFX, declares play_player_dodge_anim for miss handling. No flight-specific presentation.

3. FUNCTIONS
- signal play_player_dodge_anim.
- play_attack_sound()/play_hit_sound(): Random "Attack N"/"Attack Hit N".
- play_draw_sound()/put_away_attack_sound(): BattlefieldInfo.combat_ai_unit.UnitInventory.current_item_equipped SFX.
- play_miss_sound(): Random "Attack Miss N" + emits play_player_dodge_anim.
- play_crit_sound(): Random "Critical Hit N".
- play_death_sound(): "Death".
- death_anim_signal(): Emits death_anim_done. Driven by AnimationPlayer method tracks.

4. POSSIBLE IMPROVEMENT

- Global BattlefieldInfo.combat_ai_unit coupling.
