FILE: scenes/camera/movement_camera.gd

1. CLASS
- No class_name. extends Camera2D.
- A secondary/auxiliary battlefield camera (a minimal companion to MainCamera). Not an autoload; a scene node. Does not register itself on BattlefieldInfo.

2. WHAT IT DOES
- On load, simply clamps its own scroll limits to the map size. It is a bare-bones camera whose only job is to bound itself to the loaded map dimensions (likely used for a specific view such as unit-movement framing).

3. FUNCTIONS
- _ready(): Sets limit_bottom = BattlefieldInfo.map_height * Cell.CELL_SIZE and limit_right = BattlefieldInfo.map_width * Cell.CELL_SIZE. Depends on BattlefieldInfo.map_width/map_height and Cell.CELL_SIZE. No signals, no other logic.

4. POSSIBLE IMPROVEMENT
- Reads BattlefieldInfo.map_* in _ready(), which assumes the map is already loaded — racy; should react to a map-loaded signal (as MainCamera does via _on_Level_mapInformationLoaded) instead of assuming order.
- No top/left limits set (only bottom/right); may allow negative scroll.
- Overlaps heavily with MainCamera; consider a shared base or a single parameterized camera.

5. RELATION TO DEATH OR TAXATION
- KEEP but likely MERGE. Trivial view-layer helper; a DoT battlefield probably needs only one clamped camera (MainCamera-style). Fold this bounding logic into the single DoT battle camera and drive limits from a map-loaded signal rather than reading a global at _ready time. Pure view; no sim implications.
