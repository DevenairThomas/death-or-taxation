SYNOPSIS — scenes/events/level_2/L2_event_part_50.gd

1. CLASS
class_name L2_Event_Part5, extends "res://scenes/events/event_base.gd" (Event_Base).
Concrete scripted cutscene beat — Level 2 final intro beat (soldiers shout, camera settles, gameplay begins).

2. WHAT IT DOES
The shortest L2 beat and the last of the intro chain: shows three soldier rally-cry lines, then pans the camera to its gameplay position and completes — handing control to the player. Pure text + camera, no movement or combat.

3. FUNCTIONS
- var dialogue (Array): three "Soldiers@portrait@text" battle cries.
- _init(): debug name/part (mislabeled "Before Battle Event"/"Part 3") + path.
- start():
  * Connects message_system.no_more_text -> move_camera.
  * enable_text(dialogue) (no set_position).
- move_camera(): tweens main_game_camera to Vector2(48,0) via the child "Tween"; connects tween_all_completed -> event_complete. When this event_done fires and empties the Event_System queue, start_events_queue triggers start_level()/resume and gameplay begins.

Coupling: BattlefieldInfo, message_system, main_game_camera+Tween. Advanced by Event_System.
