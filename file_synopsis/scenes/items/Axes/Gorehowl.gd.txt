SYNOPSIS: scenes/items/Axes/Gorehowl.gd

1. CLASS
No class_name; extends "res://scenes/items/Item.gd" (Item / Node2D). Concrete weapon
script for Gorehowl.tscn — a named/boss "legendary" axe (flavor: Vezarius' axe).

2. WHAT IT DOES
A powerful unique axe. _ready() sets uses/max_uses 45, might 20 (very high), weight 15,
hit 85, crit 30, range 2-1 (max_range 2, min_range 1 — can strike at range), worth 100,
weapon_type AXE, strong_against LANCE, icon gorehowl.png, weapon_string_name "axe". Flavor
text ties it to a character named Vezarius. Not in Item_Dict.all_items (unique/boss content,
not shop-available).

3. FUNCTIONS
- _ready(): assigns stats; notable for might 20, crit 30, and 1-2 range on an axe.
- draw_attack_sound() / put_away_attack_sound(): standard draw/put-away SFX from
  BattlefieldInfo.weapon_sounds.
- special_ability(unit_holding_this_item, unit_that_is_being_attacked): returns 1 (params
  unused) — despite being a "legendary" weapon, it has NO special effect implemented; its
  power is purely stat inflation.

4. POSSIBLE IMPROVEMENT
- A named legendary weapon is the ideal place for a real special_ability, yet it returns 1.
  Either implement its gimmick or acknowledge it's stat-only.
- worth 100 on a legendary is likely a placeholder/mistake vs its power; sell-value guard
  should exclude uniques.
- Data-drive like all other weapons.

5. RELATION TO DEATH OR TAXATION
SCRAPPED as an item, but conceptually the CLOSEST bridge to DoT's design. A unique, named,
character-bound weapon with distinctive power maps onto DoT's named GENERALS and their
per-General abilities. In DoT, "Vezarius' signature power" would not be a weapon — it would
be a General ability defined in /data, unlocked at L3/L5, applied as a multiplicative
modifier in the single damage pipeline (exactly the role special_ability was meant to fill
but here left as a stub). The crit 30 is banned (no RNG). Keep the "named character has a
signature ability" idea; scrap the item and its stats.
