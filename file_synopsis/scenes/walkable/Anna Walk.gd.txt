SYNOPSIS: scenes/walkable/Anna Walk.gd

1. CLASS
extends CharacterBody2D. No class_name. An idle NPC avatar in the walkable camp map. Anna is
the SHOP/PREP-screen NPC — talking to her opens the shop rather than starting VN dialogue.

2. WHAT IT DOES
Anna stands idle in the camp and, when the player talks to her, transitions the game from the
free-roam walkable map into the preparation/shop screen. She is the bridge NPC between the
overworld camp and the prep/deployment UI.

3. FUNCTIONS
_ready() — plays "Idle" animation.
start_dialogue() — invoked by Eirka_Walk on interact. It: sets the Shop node's
  came_from_walkable_map = true (so the shop knows to return to the map on exit); stops the
  walkable map's music (BattlefieldInfo.walkable_map.music.stop()); sets the player avatar's
  status to UI (BattlefieldInfo.walkable_map.eirika_walk.current_status = Eirka_Walk.STATUS.UI)
  to freeze movement; and calls the Shop's start() to open the prep/shop screen.
Autoloads/classes touched: BattlefieldInfo (preparation_screen -> "Shop" node, walkable_map ->
  music + eirika_walk), Eirka_Walk (STATUS.UI enum). No movement/patrol logic.

4. POSSIBLE IMPROVEMENT
- start_dialogue() reaches four levels deep through globals
  (BattlefieldInfo.preparation_screen.get_node("Shop"), BattlefieldInfo.walkable_map.music,
  .eirika_walk) — highly coupled train-wreck access; a single "open shop" signal or a camp
  manager method would decouple it.
- Overloads the start_dialogue() name (same as talk NPCs) to mean "open shop"; a distinct
  interaction type/flag would be clearer.
- Depends on get_node("Shop") string path and the came_from_walkable_map flag on the shop.

5. RELATION TO DEATH OR TAXATION
This is the camp -> prep/shop TRANSITION, conceptually relevant to DoT even though the
implementation is scrapped. DoT has a between-missions economy (souls spent on leveling/
reviving Generals: costs 30/60/100/150/210; revival = 18 x level) accessed from a camp/prep
screen — Anna is the reference project's "enter the prep screen" entry point. KEEP THE CONCEPT
(a clear hand-off from camp into deployment/economy UI, remembering where to return), SCRAP the
implementation (deep global reach-through, real-time avatar freeze, hardcoded node paths). In
DoT this transition should be a signal/scene-change through a proper flow controller, and the
"shop" is DoT's soul economy screen, not an FE item shop. Note DoT currency rules: souls are
spent only between missions, budget never carries over. Nothing here informs the /sim grid.
