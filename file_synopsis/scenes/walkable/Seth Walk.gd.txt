SYNOPSIS: scenes/walkable/Seth Walk.gd

1. CLASS
extends CharacterBody2D. No class_name. An idle NPC party-member avatar in the walkable camp
map (Seth, a cavalier). Has a talk_name = "Seth" field.

2. WHAT IT DOES
Seth stands idle in the camp and delivers a scripted VN dialogue when talked to. His lines are
exposition about the training grounds / camp itself: what the prep area is for (training units,
preparations, alliances) and why it currently looks empty (no builders/resources yet) — a
lightweight world/tutorial beat. Content is a hardcoded `dialogue` array in
"Name@portrait_path@text" format. No movement or patrol.

3. FUNCTIONS
_ready() — plays "Idle" animation.
start_dialogue() — invoked by Eirka_Walk on interact; calls
  BattlefieldInfo.message_system.start(dialogue) to display Seth's 5-line conversation.
Fields: talk_name = "Seth" (an interaction-label field also present on Colm; unused by the
  message call itself here).
Autoloads/classes touched: BattlefieldInfo.message_system; called by the Eirka_Walk avatar.

4. POSSIBLE IMPROVEMENT
- Hardcoded dialogue + portrait paths belong in /data, not in the script.
- Structurally identical to Naomi/Vanessa Walk — one shared data-driven TalkNPC would replace
  all three. talk_name exists here but not on Naomi/Vanessa — inconsistent NPC interface.
- No class_name; the reusable "idle NPC that talks" pattern should be a named base class.

5. RELATION TO DEATH OR TAXATION
Camp NPC exposition, not tactical-grid logic — outside the grid-builder mandate; SCRAP the
per-character-script form. Transferable idea: content-as-data, plus this specific dialogue is
essentially the camp's self-description — DoT's camp/prep screen (between-mission soul economy:
level/revive Generals) could use a similar in-character explainer, but data-defined and in
DoT's voice (the earnest lich). Note DoT rules differ from the FE fiction here: DoT's
"training/preparation" is soul spending between missions only, no mid-mission economy, no budget
carry-over. Rebuild any such NPC as a shared VN component reading /data dialogue. Nothing here
informs /sim.
