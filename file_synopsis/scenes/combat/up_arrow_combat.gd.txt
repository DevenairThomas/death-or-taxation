SYNOPSIS: scenes/combat/up_arrow_combat.gd

1. CLASS
   extends Node2D (no class_name). Decorative widget: the green "up" arrow shown next to a combatant's stats when they have a weapon-triangle ADVANTAGE (weapon_bonus == 1). Mirror of down_arrow_combat.gd.

2. WHAT IT DOES
   Plays a "move" (bobbing) animation on entering the tree. Visibility toggled by combat.gd.adjust_gui_text_and_hp_box() from Combat_Calculator.*_weapon_bonus.

3. FUNCTIONS
   _ready(): $anim.play("move"). No signals or logic.

4. POSSIBLE IMPROVEMENT
   - Replaceable by AnimationPlayer autoplay; script is redundant.

5. RELATION TO DEATH OR TAXATION
   SCRAP as-is. Same reasoning as down_arrow_combat.gd — it advertises the FE weapon triangle, which DoT does not use. No reusable logic to port.
